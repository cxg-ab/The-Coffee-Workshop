# The Coffee Workshop Co. — Brand Guidelines

> Derived from the approved brand assets and project plan.

## Logo

- **Primary:** Bronze/coffee brown on white background
- **Source files:** `tcw logo.pdf`, `assets/brand/logo-primary.png`
- **Clear space:** Minimum padding equal to height of "CO." in wordmark
- **Do not:** Stretch, recolor to non-brand hues, add effects, place on low-contrast photos

## Color palette

| Name | Hex | CSS variable |

**Mood:** White luxury, bronze accents, bold, simple.

## Typography

| Role | Font | Style |
|------|------|--------|
| Display / headings | DM Sans | Bold, uppercase, wide letter-spacing |
| Body | DM Sans | Regular/medium, comfortable line-height |
| Arabic | Noto Sans Arabic | Matches visual weight of EN |

## Photography (when available)

- Warm, natural light
- Product-focused; shallow depth of field for beans
- Show roast texture, origin context, brewing ritual
- Avoid stock-looking generic coffee cup clichés

## Voice

| Do | Don't |
|----|--------|
| Specialty, micro-lot, small-batch, master roasters | "Best coffee ever" hype |
| RAK, UAE, farmer relationships | Vague global claims |
| Personable, honest, dedicated | Corporate jargon |
| Bilingual parity (EN + AR) | Machine-translated AR |

## E-commerce scope

**Online:** Beans (Arabica) + accessories/equipment  
**In-store café:** Food menu — mention on Visit Us section only
